## 📖 About

You have a Computer Science degree. You have ambition. You have absolutely no professional experience.

Welcome to JOB QUEST, an endless game inspired by the absurd reality of searching for your first job in the technology industry.

Run, jump, collect CVs, drink coffee and dodge an endless stream of rejection letters.

**Your objective is simple: Get a job.**

Unfortunately, that's easier said than done.


> 🤖 **AI-NATIVE PROJECT**
>
> Conceptualised, developed and iterated with AI assistance (with a single prompt).
>
> Built through human creativity, AI collaboration and a questionable number of rejected job applications.
>
> **Human ambition. Artificial intelligence. Zero employment.**

## 🕹️ Gameplay

- Run and jump through an endless environment.
- Collect CVs to gain experience points.
- Collect coffee for temporary motivation.
- Dodge rejection letters and impossible job requirements.
- Survive as long as possible while difficulty increases.
- Beat your personal distance record.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / ← | Move left |
| D / → | Move right |
| SPACE / ↑ | Jump |

Mobile touch controls are also supported.

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Browser Local Storage

No frameworks, external game engines or installation required.
